06 / Layout

One container, three widths

Every page is checked at 390, 768 and 1280 px. Values below are the ones the home page uses.

Container

Maximum width 1200 px, centred. Text blocks sit inside it at 34 to 46 em.

Gutter

clamp(20px, 4vw, 48px) on both sides.

Section padding

clamp(56px, 8vw, 96px) above and below. Light sections are divided by a 1 px paper-300 rule. An Ink band divides itself.

Cards

Flex-wrap with a 24 px gap, each card at least 300 px wide. White surface, 1 px paper-300 border, 28 px padding, square corners.

Radius

2 px on buttons and inputs. Nothing else is rounded.

Chamfer

28 px cut at the top right and bottom left of image frames and one hero panel, at 45 degrees. Never on buttons or inputs.

Touch targets

Primary button 52 px high, header button 44 px, inputs 48 px, checkbox 24 px inside a 44 px row.

Header

Lockup at 32 px symbol height above 768 px and 28 px below. On a phone the header keeps the lockup and one button, with no menu.
