10 / Devices

Trace, pad ring and section drawing

All devices come from the logo and sit at 45 degrees. The trace appears once per page, in the process list. The hatch is a drawing convention for cut material and never a background pattern.

Trace

  1. Describe the product in the form, in words.

  2. Get a written scope. Deliverables and price.

  3. Hand off to manufacturers.

A 2 px Ink line, 45 degree jogs and pad rings. Vertical on every width.

Pad ring

The hole is 40 percent of the outer diameter, as in the logo pads. In use the ring is 24 px, with the stroke in Ink.

Section hatch

Marks cut material in section drawings only. As wallpaper it fights the text, even at 12 percent.

Drawing style

  • Ink lines 1.5 to 2 px, Steel dimension lines 1 px, one Signal callout. Each part is hatched in its own direction, both at 45 degrees.
  • Labels are HTML in Plex Mono at text-sm, never text inside a scaled drawing. A legend sits under the figure.
  • Drawn parts: real work or a clearly labelled illustration. No stock images, gears or bulbs.
  • One subject on a Paper or Ink ground, with a scale cue where a photo is used.

11 / Rules

Do and do not

Do

  • Let Ink and Paper carry the page.
  • Take every colour from the tokens.
  • Use the tile under 24 px and a solid panel under the logo on photos.
  • Show real work and mark gaps [TO CONFIRM].

Do not

  • Set Signal text on Paper or white, white text on Signal, or Steel for small text.
  • Alter the logo.
  • Use gradients, stock photos, generated imagery or hatch wallpaper.
  • Hard-code the name outside src/config/site.ts.
