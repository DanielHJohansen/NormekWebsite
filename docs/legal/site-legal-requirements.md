## 2. Launch and first customer

| Issue and why | What to do | Who signs off, and when |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Company details.** E-commerce act s7 expects name, street address, email (a form alone is not enough) and CVR number. Companies Act s2 repeats this for companies. | Footer and `/privacy/`: legal name as in CVR including form, CVR, street address, email. A sole proprietor's CVR address is public, so consider a business address. | Daniel, accountant. Launch (blocker). |
| **2. Privacy notice** (GDPR Arts. 12 to 14). | Use `privacy-notice-draft.md`. Under the form button: "I use your details only to answer this request. Cloudflare, Resend and Simply.com handle them for me. See the privacy notice for retention and your rights." | **Lawyer.** Launch. |
| **3. Processors and transfers** (Arts. 28, 44 to 46). Each DPA must cover Art. 28(3): instructions only, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, rights and breach help, deletion or return, audits. | **Cloudflare:** DPA is part of its terms. **Resend:** accept the DPA, choose Ireland (eu-west-1) for the domain. Account data stays in the US. Unconfirmed: free-plan region choice, log retention (1 to 30 days) and message-text storage. If bad, Scaleway (ADR A5). **Simply.com:** accept the DPA in the control panel, data is in Denmark. **Transfers:** Resend says it joined the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (2025), Cloudflare appears listed. Verify on dataprivacyframework.gov. An appeal (C-703/25 P) was pending at last check, so keep standard contractual clauses as fallback. Re-accept if a company is formed. | Daniel, **lawyer** reads once. Before testers submit. |
| **4. Cookies and Turnstile.** ePrivacy Art. 5(3) and the Danish cookie order cover any storage or device access (EDPB 2/2023). The site sets none. Turnstile reads IP, user agent and TLS fingerprint and sets a cookie only with optional pre-clearance, off by default. Local storage: unknown. "Strictly necessary" is arguable. | Run the browser test. Load Turnstile on first focus of a form field, not on scroll (ADR A6 change). If consent is needed: honeypot, rate limit, email address, no widget. For analytics (ADR 0002), update the notice and ask the lawyer about a banner. | **Lawyer**, security-engineer. Launch. |
| **5. Retention, rights, breaches.** Accounting records: 5 years (Bookkeeping Act s12). No statutory period for unanswered inquiries, so Daniel picks one he will keep. | Proposed: delete inquiries 12 months after last contact, hashed IP 1 hour `[TO CONFIRM built]`. Answer rights requests within one month (Art. 12(3)). Breach: tell Datatilsynet within 72 hours unless no risk (Art. 33). Keep a short record of processing (Art. 30, narrow exemption). | Daniel. Launch. |
| **6. Marketing claims.** Marketing Practices Act s3 (also B2B), s20 (misleading practice between traders), s21 (comparisons). Directive 2006/114/EC covers the advertiser's qualifications. | **"Senior"** is undefined but a qualification claim. State facts (B.Eng. 2021, SkyGrids 2022 to 2025). **Implied team:** avoid "we", "our engineers", "our partners". Use "I" or Daniel's name. **Doubling ranges** ("2 to 4 weeks", DKK 30,000 to 60,000): label as estimates with assumptions, say "fixed price" only after a written scope, add a ledger row each. | Daniel, content-copywriter. Launch. |
| **7. Do not claim** on the privacy page or footer. | "GDPR compliant", "certified", "secure", "encrypted", "data stays in the EU", "never shared", "no cookies" until tested, retention not kept, "ApS", CVR or "insured" until true, "team". | Daniel. Launch. |
| **8. AI and customer files.** No public AI statement, so nothing to substantiate. Vague AI lines need C-002, C-003 evidence and a lawyer check if they return. NDAs increasingly restrict generative AI. | (a) If an NDA or customer asks about AI use, answer honestly and fully. (b) Customer files never go into free or personal AI accounts. (c) Update the notice and DPAs before inquiry text goes into any other tool. (d) A chatbot or AI-generated media would trigger AI Act Art. 50. | Daniel, **lawyer** for NDA wording. Before the first customer file. |
| **9. Testimonials and past work.** Testimonials praise Daniel's earlier personal work. Past-employer work is not ours to publish. | Written permission per person (a name is personal data), labelled "work done before Normek", or drop. Same per work item (`docs/content-reuse-inventory.md`). The lawyer reads the real SkyGrids share sale terms, employment contracts and NDAs (confidentiality, IP, non-compete, non-solicit). Log licences in `docs/media-manifest.md`. | Daniel, **lawyer**. Launch, per item. |
| **10. Accessibility.** The European Accessibility Act covers consumer-facing services and exempts microenterprises (under 10 people, turnover or balance sheet up to EUR 2 million, Art. 4(5)). A B2B information site is probably outside on both counts. | Build to WCAG 2.2 AA anyway. Recheck if Normek sells to consumers. | qa-accessibility-engineer. Later. |
| **11. Insurance and product role.** Design errors mostly cause pure financial loss (professional indemnity). One Danish architects and engineers policy excludes projects where the adviser also helped carry them out, which matters once Normek supplies parts. | Get broker quotes and state the parts plan. Fix Normek's role per project in writing (designer, not manufacturer or CE signatory). Say "insured" only once a policy exists. New EU product liability rules start 9 December 2026 `[verify]`. | Broker, **lawyer**. Before the first customer. |
| **12. Name and trademark (blocker).** Company names must be clearly distinguishable from registered businesses (Companies Act s2). Status of NORMEK A/S (CVR 29423431) and whether a different legal-form suffix suffices are unconfirmed. Prior use since 2006 can give rights without registration (Marketing Practices Act s22). Trademark confusion includes sound-alikes (EU Regulation 2017/1001 Art. 8(1)(b)). | (1) datacvr.virk.dk: search "normek" and 29423431. Ask Erhvervsstyrelsen if "Normek ApS" would be refused. (2) Search NORMEK, NORMEC and sound-alikes at the Danish Patent and Trademark Office, EUIPO TMview, WIPO and in Finland, classes 6, 7, 9, 37, 40, 42. (3) Get a trademark attorney's written opinion: proceed, seek NORMEK A/S's consent, or rename. | **Trademark attorney**, Daniel. Launch (blocker). |
| **13. Contracts and outreach.** Cold outreach has its own rules (Marketing Practices Act s10, GDPR Art. 14) `[verify]`. | Lawyer drafts the mutual NDA and services agreement (IP assignment of rights Normek holds, no warranty that all output is protectable, liability cap, partner pass-through, tooling ownership, file deletion) and outreach rules. | **Lawyer.** Before the first customer and first cold message. |
