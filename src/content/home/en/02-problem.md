    text: A sealed box breathes. When it cools, the air inside contracts and pulls humid air in through the weakest seal. A membrane vent equalizes the pressure and keeps out liquid water and dust, but it passes water vapor, so condensation can still form. I start with the cheapest fix that works.

    text: Printing suits prototypes and small batches. As volume grows into the hundreds and beyond, casting or molding can cost less per part. Tooling only pays off once volume covers it, and where that happens depends on the part, so I ask for quotes at your volumes.
