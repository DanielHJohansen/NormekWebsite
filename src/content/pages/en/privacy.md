## What happens when you send the form

1. Your browser sends the form to a small program on Cloudflare's network.
2. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person, not a bot.
3. The program emails your message to daniel@normek.dk through Resend, with your address as reply-to.
4. I read it in my mailbox at Simply.com and reply from there.

There is no separate inquiry database. Copies sit in my mailbox and, briefly, Resend's log.

## What I collect

- **What you type:** name, work email address, company and message [TO CONFIRM against the final form, incl. campaign code].
- **Bot check:** Turnstile receives your IP address and browser signals to decide whether you are a person. I get only the result.
- **Any page visit:** Cloudflare, the host, processes your IP address and request details (page, time, browser) to deliver and protect it [TO CONFIRM: function logs off].
- **Direct email:** your address, name and what you write.

Send no confidential files or sensitive personal data. Files come after a non-disclosure agreement.

## Why, and on what legal basis

- **Answering your inquiry**, and quoting work if you ask: legitimate interest in answering business inquiries (Article 6(1)(f) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). For a sole trader asking for a quote, also steps before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
- **Keeping the form free of spam and abuse:** legitimate interest in securing the site and mailbox (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Contracts, invoices and accounts** if we work together: contract (Article 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation under the Danish Bookkeeping Act (Article 6(1)(c)).

Giving details is voluntary, but I cannot reply without a name and email address. I do not use them for marketing and make no automated decisions about you.
